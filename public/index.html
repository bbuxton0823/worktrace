The market

It's hands.

The system

Capture in the
flow of work.

01

Wear

A small camera in a hat brim points at the hands. The worker just works.

02

Track

21 landmarks per hand, tracked live, with a hands-visible meter that catches bad framing before it costs an hour.

03

Label

Task chips segment the episode as it happens. Quality is scored before anyone leaves the site.

The wedge

Start where
consent is clean.

Home staging

Vacant houses. Furniture carries, assembly, styling. A crew of two produces synchronized multi-view episodes of every task.

Rental turnover

Empty short-term rentals on a schedule. The same tasks, hundreds of times, across hundreds of rooms.

Hotel floors

Standardized rooms worldwide. A floor in Manila trains the same robot as a floor in Chicago.

Offshore arms researched and ranked: the Philippines first, Colombia second. Occupied homes come last, once the privacy pipeline has earned it.

The product

Annotations and rights.
Not raw footage.

  • World-frame camera pose from the rig's own telemetry
  • 3D hand pose with per-joint confidence
  • Language-labeled task segments
  • A signed consent record inside every episode manifest
  • Delivered in the formats labs actually train on

Raw video is nearly free. Provenance is not.
